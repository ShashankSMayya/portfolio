Privacy Policy - Dota 2 Soundboard

Effective date: August 9, 2026 · App: com.shashankmayya.dota2soundboard · Developer: Shashank S Mayya

Dota 2 Soundboard (the "app") is a free Android app for playing Dota 2 voice lines and sounds. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app requires no account and no sign-in, and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Data collected automatically

The app uses Google services for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising. These services automatically collect:

  • Usage data: in-app events and screen views (for example: a sound was played, a pack was downloaded, a quiz was completed), collected by Google Analytics for Firebase. Search text and other free-form input are never included in analytics events.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: stack traces, device model, operating system version, and similar diagnostic information, collected by Firebase Crashlytics when the app crashes or malfunctions.
  • Device and other identifiers: the Android Advertising ID, a per-install Firebase app-instance ID, and similar identifiers, used by Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Approximate location: a coarse, city-level location derived by Google from your device's IP address. The app itself never requests any location permission and has no access to your precise location.

Data you provide

If you report an incorrect sound from inside the app, the report (a reason chosen from a fixed list and an optional short note you type) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore together with an identifier of the sound being reported. Reports are not linked to your identity, and submitting one is entirely optional.

How the data is used

  • Analytics: to understand which features are used and to improve the app.
  • Stability: to find and fix crashes and errors.
  • Advertising: to show a single banner ad, served by Google AdMob, which funds the app. AdMob may use the Advertising ID and IP address to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads.
  • Content fixes: wrong-sound reports tell us exactly which sound to fix.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google as the provider of Firebase and AdMob. Advertising-related data (Advertising ID, IP address, ad interactions) is used by Google's advertising network to serve and measure ads. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any other third party. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads / Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Data deletion: the app has no accounts, so there is no profile to delete. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device or of a report you submitted, contact us at the address below.

Security and retention

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics for a limited period (14 months by default) before automatic deletion; crash reports and sound reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the underlying problems. Sounds you download and preferences you set (favourites, theme, play history) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when adding new features, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
